What is Artificial Intelligence?

Definition

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science concerned with building systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence. These tasks include recognising patterns, understanding natural language, making decisions, and generating novel content. AI systems learn from data, identify structure in that data, and use what they have learned to produce useful outputs or take actions in the world.

Modern AI is often divided into narrow AI, which excels at specific tasks such as image classification or language translation, and general AI, which remains a long-term research goal aimed at human-level reasoning across domains.

Brief History

  • 1950 — Turing Test: Alan Turing proposes the "imitation game" as a criterion for machine intelligence, laying the conceptual foundation for the field.
  • 1970s–1980s — Expert Systems: Rule-based systems such as MYCIN and XCON demonstrate that encoded human expertise can solve narrow professional problems, driving the first wave of commercial AI.
  • 1990s–2000s — Machine Learning Renaissance: Statistical methods, support vector machines, and ensemble techniques shift the field from hand-crafted rules to data-driven learning at scale.
  • 2012 — Deep Learning Breakthrough (AlexNet): A deep convolutional neural network wins the ImageNet competition by a large margin, proving that deep architectures trained on GPUs can outperform classical methods on perception tasks.
  • 2017 — Transformer Architecture: The "Attention Is All You Need" paper introduces the Transformer, replacing recurrence with self-attention and enabling massive parallelism that powers modern language models.
  • 2020 — GPT-3: OpenAI demonstrates that scaling language models to 175 billion parameters produces emergent few-shot learning capabilities across diverse tasks.
  • 2022 — ChatGPT: A conversational interface to large language models reaches widespread public adoption, making AI accessible to non-specialists for the first time.
  • 2023+ — Claude and Copilot: Anthropic's Claude and GitHub Copilot advance AI-assisted coding and reasoning, enabling developers to collaborate with AI agents directly inside their workflows.

Use Cases

AI is applied across virtually every industry. Representative real-world applications include:

  • Healthcare: Medical image analysis, drug discovery, and clinical decision support systems.
  • Finance: Fraud detection, algorithmic trading, and automated credit scoring.
  • Transportation: Autonomous vehicles, route optimisation, and predictive maintenance for fleets.
  • Natural Language Processing: Machine translation, document summarisation, and conversational assistants.
  • AI Coding Agents: Tools like GitHub Copilot and Claude act as AI coding agents — autonomous assistants that read project context, generate code, run tests, and iterate on solutions alongside human developers.
